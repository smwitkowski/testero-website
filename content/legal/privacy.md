## Information we use

- **Accounts:** your email address and authentication information are processed through Supabase Auth. We use your account identifier to keep your study activity associated with your account.
- **Diagnostics and practice:** we store sessions, question snapshots, selected answers, correctness, scores, domains and activity times to provide results and progress. Without an account, a browser cookie connects you to your diagnostic; this is pseudonymous, not untraceable activity. When you sign in, eligible browser-owned diagnostics can be linked to your account.
- **Requested results emails:** if you ask us to email your report, we record the email address, session, consent, delivery status and a copy of the message in Supabase. Resend processes the recipient and report content for delivery. This request does not subscribe you to a newsletter or reminders.
- **Question reports:** we store the question/session reference, report reason, your note, optional contact email and account identifier or browser-token hash so we can investigate. Avoid including sensitive personal information in notes.
- **Certification waitlists:** we store your email, certification interest and, when supplied, source page and campaign information to record your interest and support availability updates.
- **Payments:** Stripe handles hosted checkout and card details. Testero does not collect card fields directly. We store billing identifiers, payment status, amount, currency, receipt links and access/refund records to deliver purchases and handle support.
- **Technical information:** our hosting and service providers process network and request information to deliver and secure the service. Abuse controls temporarily use hashed IP-derived identifiers; the infrastructure still processes network addresses.

## Cookies, analytics and session recording

Necessary cookies keep you signed in and connect an unsigned diagnostic to your browser. The diagnostic cookie lasts up to 30 days; we store a hash of its random token on the server. Cookie expiry does not delete stored results. Blocking these cookies can prevent sign-in or access to your diagnostic.

When analytics is configured, PostHog uses cookies and local storage to measure page visits, study activity and conversion events. Analytics can be associated with your account identifier. Session replay is configured for diagnostic, practice, dashboard, account and checkout pages, with input and page-text masking. Masking reduces exposure but is not a promise that all personal data is excluded.
