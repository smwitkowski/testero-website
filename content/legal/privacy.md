## Who we are

Testero ("we", "us") provides independent certification study tools at testero.ai. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. Contact [support@testero.ai](mailto:support@testero.ai) with privacy questions or requests.

## Information we use

- **Accounts:** your email address and authentication information are processed through Supabase Auth. We use your account identifier to keep your study activity associated with your account.
- **Diagnostics and practice:** we store sessions, question snapshots, selected answers, correctness, scores, domains and activity times to provide results and progress. Without an account, a browser cookie connects you to your diagnostic; this is pseudonymous, not untraceable activity. When you sign in, eligible browser-owned diagnostics can be linked to your account.
- **Requested results emails:** if you ask us to email your report, we record the email address, session, consent, delivery status and a copy of the message. Resend processes the recipient and report content for delivery. This request does not subscribe you to a newsletter or reminders.
- **Question reports:** we store the question/session reference, report reason, your note, optional contact email and account identifier or browser-token hash so we can investigate. Avoid including sensitive personal information in notes.
- **Certification waitlists:** we store your email, certification interest and, when supplied, source page and campaign information to record your interest and send availability updates.
- **Payments:** Stripe handles hosted checkout and card details. Testero does not collect card fields directly. We store billing identifiers, payment status, amount, currency, receipt links and access/refund records to deliver purchases and handle support.
- **Technical information:** our hosting and service providers process network and request information to deliver and secure the service. Abuse controls temporarily use hashed IP-derived identifiers.

## Cookies, analytics and session recording

Necessary cookies keep you signed in and connect a diagnostic taken without an account to your browser. The diagnostic cookie lasts up to 30 days; we store a hash of its random token on the server. Cookie expiry does not delete stored results. Blocking these cookies can prevent sign-in or access to your diagnostic.

We use PostHog analytics, which uses cookies and local storage to measure page visits, study activity and conversion events. Analytics can be associated with your account identifier. Session replay may run on diagnostic, practice, dashboard, account and checkout pages, with input and page-text masking. Masking reduces exposure but does not guarantee that all personal data is excluded. You can limit analytics with browser privacy settings or content blockers.

## Why we use and share information

We use this information to provide and secure accounts, results and study tools; send requested messages; investigate reports; administer purchases and refunds; and understand and improve how the product is used. We do not sell your personal information.

Our service providers include Supabase (authentication and data), Stripe (payments), Resend (email), PostHog (analytics) and Google Cloud (hosting). Providers may process information outside your country. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the service and its users.

## Retention and security

We keep records for as long as needed to operate the service, support users, prevent abuse and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. A pass expiring, a diagnostic session expiring or a cookie being removed does not by itself delete server records. We use access controls and ownership checks, but no online service can promise complete security.

## Your choices and requests

You can use the diagnostic without an account, and you choose whether to request an email, join a waitlist or add a contact email to a report. Browser controls can remove cookies and local storage, but they do not delete server records. Contact [support@testero.ai](mailto:support@testero.ai) to ask about accessing, correcting or deleting your information. We may need to verify that the account is yours, and we may keep records required for legal, payment or abuse-prevention purposes. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and you can contact us to use them.

## Children

Testero is intended for adults preparing for professional certifications. It is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes. Material changes may also be communicated through the service or by email where appropriate.
