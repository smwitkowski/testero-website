## Assumptions and limits
- New limiter constants: **5 attempts / forwarded-address bucket /60s**, **100 total attempts / instance /60s**. Duplicates and honeypots consume attempts. This is deliberately best effort, not a distributed quota or trusted-IP identity: headers can be spoofed, one caller can exhaust shared instance capacity, instances/restarts have independent budgets. Aggregate cap bounds rotated-header bucket creation. No raw IP is stored. Stronger edge control needs a separately verified deployment/proxy policy.
- Notifications mean diagnostic launch only, not general marketing or a promised date. No email is sent by this PR. Production migration is the coordinator's post-merge responsibility; final legal pages remain the existing D-032 placeholders.
